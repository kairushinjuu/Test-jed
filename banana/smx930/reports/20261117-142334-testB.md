## Causal conclusions and non-conclusions
1. **No meaningful reduction of audio cuts or recurring Vulkan submission overhead** from the two-switch Test B relative to the earlier intro. The earlier 13:35 baseline auto-selected GPU-signalled timelines and used two async workers; Test B explicitly selected GPU-signalled timelines and one worker. Actual concurrent compilation was already capped at **one** by `DM_VK_PSO_SLOTS=1` in both runs. However, the baseline had cold-cache misses while Test B showed 17 hits / 0 misses, and the observation windows are different, so no causal speedup can be established. Relative to the separate 14:03 crash run, two tuning controls changed.
2. The prior **heavy 3D** frame in the 14:03 log required 72 pipeline creations / 24.6 seconds of compilation and allocated ~3.1 GiB. The **new run ends during the intro**, so it neither reproduces nor resolves that separate post-intro problem.
3. `DM_VK_CHUNK` / `DM_VK_CHUNK_PASS` defaults are 600 draws; the intro has roughly 2–5 draws per frame, yet six queue submissions, implying synchronization/part boundaries rather than draw-count chunking. Do **not** disable NVN barriers or coalesce submissions without proving ordering and fence/timeline correctness.
4. Audio buffering can mask short underruns but cannot prevent a >1-second GPU fence wait. The log's 3,840-frame AAudio buffer is 80 ms at 48 kHz.
